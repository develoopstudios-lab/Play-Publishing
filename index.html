FAQs

Common Questions

Important information about Google Play Console and publishing support.

Can Develoop Studios guarantee Google Play Console approval?

No. Google controls account verification, developer-account decisions and app publishing reviews. We can help explain requirements and troubleshoot legitimate issues, but approval cannot be guaranteed.

Can you help with a delayed verification process?

We can help you review visible status messages and instructions, check that submitted details are consistent, and identify appropriate next steps. Processing times and outcomes depend on Google.

Do you help with organization developer accounts?

Yes. We can provide guidance on published organization-account requirements and help you prepare accurate business information and supporting documents. The account must belong to the real organization.

Can you help me obtain a D‑U‑N‑S Number?

We can help you find official D‑U‑N‑S lookup or application resources and understand the steps. Any identifier used should be valid and associated with the correct business.

What information should I send when contacting you?

Describe your goal, the type of app or account, and the stage where you need help. Do not send passwords, one-time verification codes, payment details or other sensitive credentials.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes, inquiries from Pakistan and international clients are welcome. Availability, scope, communication and payment terms should be confirmed before work begins.
